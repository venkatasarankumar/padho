Our Mission & Philosophy

Democratizing Competitive Exam Education for Every Student.

PADHO is a free digital platform built to solve the fragmentation, commercialization, and linguistic exclusion that students encounter when preparing for major competitive examinations.

The Real Problem

Why We Built PADHO

Millions of students across India complete 10th standard and Intermediate with the ambition of entering engineering, government administration, banking, or railway careers. However, they encounter severe hurdles:

  • • Scattered Information: Crucial eligibility rules and syllabus notices are dispersed across confusing government portals.
  • • Commercial Paywalls: Commercial institutes charge exorbitant fees for materials that are essentially based on open NCERT textbooks.
  • • Language Barriers: Talented students studying in vernacular mediums like Telugu often lack structured, high-quality preparation materials in their language.

Our Core Commitments

100% Free Forever

Education is a fundamental right. PADHO does not charge students, gatekeep content, or sell private user data.

Zero Fabricated Information

We never invent fake examination notifications, artificial student leaderboards, or false statistics. Every resource is verified.

Bilingual Equity

Dedicated support for both English and Telugu mediums, ensuring conceptual clarity without linguistic disadvantage.

Who PADHO Serves

Designed for Ambitious Aspirants

Tailored specifically for students embarking on their foundational competitive journey.

Students After 10th Standard

Guidance on stream selection, polytechnic routes, foundational NCERT concepts, and early competitive awareness.

Intermediate (11th & 12th) Students

In-depth preparation for JEE Main, JEE Advanced, and early central clerical/railway opportunities.

Government & Banking Aspirants

Syllabus-aligned preparation for SSC CGL, CHSL, IBPS, SBI PO, and RRB recruitments with genuine practice materials.
